Built an **AI-powered cancer support chatbot** using **FastAPI**, **Dialogflow ES**, and **Gemini LLM** to provide insights on **symptoms, treatments, and drugs**, and recommend **relevant research papers**.  
Integrated **TF-IDF**, **Doc2Vec**, and **KMeans clustering** for research retrieval and recommendation.

Key integrations include:

- **UMLS API** — for medical terms, dosage, and side effects.
- **Mayo Clinic API** — for disease overviews, symptoms, and treatments.
- **Gemini LLM** — for clear and empathetic responses.

Implemented **multi-intent routing** in Dialogflow (drug info, symptoms, diagnosis, research help) with **MLOps pipelines** to enhance scalability and reproducibility.
